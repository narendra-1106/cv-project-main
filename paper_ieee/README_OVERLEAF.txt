HOW TO UPLOAD TO OVERLEAF:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Log into your Overleaf account (https://www.overleaf.com).
2. Click the green "New Project" button on the top left.
3. Select "Upload Project".
4. Drag and drop or browse to select the "LUXVISION_AI_IEEE_Paper.zip" file.
5. Overleaf will unpack all files automatically into a new project.
6. Ensure the compiler is set to "pdfLaTeX" (default in Overleaf Menu -> Settings).
7. Click "Recompile" (Ctrl + Enter) to generate the camera-ready IEEE PDF!
